Production work

Before moving into investment products, I spent several years in film, television, and live events — most of it as a production designer, credited as Sheryl Liu. It was where I learned to run budgets and timelines, to hold a large team to a fixed delivery date, and to build something complicated that still had to read clearly to an audience.

I keep this page as a record rather than a pitch. The habits carried over; the job changed.
